Quantum Mechanics
Reality at the atomic scale is stranger than fiction. Particles exist in superposition, tunnel through barriers, and become entangled across infinite distances.
Ĥψ = iℏ ∂ψ/∂t — Schrödinger Equation
Wave-particle duality: light is both wave and particle
Heisenberg's uncertainty: Δx·Δp ≥ ℏ/2 — measurement disturbs reality
Quantum tunneling enables nuclear fusion in stars
Basis of transistors, lasers, MRI machines & quantum computers
02 / 07
🌊
Wave Optics
Light waves interfere, diffract, and polarize. These phenomena power everything from holograms to fiber optic communications to LASIK surgery.
d sin θ = mλ — Diffraction Grating
Young's double slit: proof of light's wave nature (1801)
Thin film interference makes soap bubbles colorful
Brewster's angle: complete polarization of reflected light
Rayleigh scattering explains why the sky is blue
03 / 07
🔋
Electromagnetism
Maxwell unified electricity, magnetism and light into four elegant equations. Every electronic device, motor, and radio wave obeys these laws.
∇ × B = μ₀J + μ₀ε₀ ∂E/∂t — Ampère-Maxwell
Maxwell's equations predicted light is an EM wave
Faraday's law: changing B-field induces electric current
EM spectrum: from radio waves to gamma rays
Lorentz force: F = q(E + v × B)
04 / 07
🌡️
Thermodynamics
The science of energy, heat, and entropy. The arrow of time itself is a thermodynamic phenomenon — entropy always increases.
dS ≥ dQ/T — Second Law (Clausius)
Zeroth Law: thermal equilibrium defines temperature
First Law: energy is conserved, always
Carnot efficiency: η = 1 − T_cold/T_hot
Entropy explains why ice melts but never unmelts
05 / 07
💎
Solid State Physics
The quantum mechanics of solids. Band theory explains why some materials conduct electricity, some insulate, and some are semiconductors.
E = ℏ²k²/2m* — Energy in a Band
Valence & conduction bands: the secret of semiconductors
Doping: adding impurities to control conductivity
Superconductivity: zero resistance below critical temp
Every CPU, LED & solar cell uses solid state physics
06 / 07
☢️
Nuclear Physics
The strong force binds quarks into protons and neutrons. Nuclear reactions release millions of times more energy than chemical reactions.
E = Δmc² — Mass-Energy Equivalence
Binding energy per nucleon: iron-56 is the most stable
Fission splits heavy nuclei; fusion merges light ones
Radioactive decay follows N = N₀e^(−λt)
Stars are nuclear fusion reactors burning hydrogen
07 / 07
🚀
Special Relativity
Time dilates. Length contracts. Simultaneity is relative. Einstein's 1905 paper demolished absolute space and time with one thought experiment.
t' = γ(t − vx/c²) — Lorentz Transform
Time dilation: GPS satellites need relativistic corrections
Length contraction: objects shorten in direction of motion
Nothing with mass can reach the speed of light
E=mc²: 1g of matter = 90 trillion joules of energy

Timeline

300 YEARS
OF BREAKTHROUGHS

1687
Newton's Principia Mathematica
Laws of motion and universal gravitation — the founding text of classical physics.
1801
Young's Double Slit Experiment
Light passes through two slits and creates an interference pattern — proving it's a wave.
1865
Maxwell's Equations
Four equations that unified electricity, magnetism, and light into one electromagnetic theory.
1900
Planck's Quantum Hypothesis
Energy is not continuous — it comes in discrete quanta: E = hf. Birth of quantum theory.
1905
Einstein's Annus Mirabilis
Special relativity, photoelectric effect, and Brownian motion — all in a single year.
1926
Schrödinger's Wave Equation
The equation governing quantum state evolution — the heart of modern quantum mechanics.
1945
First Nuclear Reaction (Manhattan Project)
E = mc² becomes devastatingly real. Nuclear age begins.
1947
Transistor Invented (Bell Labs)
Solid state physics creates the building block of all modern electronics.
2012
Higgs Boson Discovered (CERN)
The "God particle" confirms the Standard Model of particle physics — 50 years after prediction.
